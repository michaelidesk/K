## Key takeaways
Parc ~600k cars + ~124k commercial (derived ~2022); 4th-highest EU car density, rising; very old fleet (66% >10yrs), fed ~60-64% by used Japanese imports; ~50k vehicles/yr entering parc 2023-2025; single-season market; Europe consumer replacement +5% 2024 but below 2019; truck structurally weak; Asian import penetration rising.
