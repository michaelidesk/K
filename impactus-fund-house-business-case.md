## The core thesis

Impactus today is a regulated, DFI-validated platform with ~€0.6m p.a. of fee income and its value trapped at trough marks. The route to a world-class fund house runs through Project V: the succession buy-out of Partners A, C and D (~€13m at today's marks) consolidates the management company, the GP layer and the anchor stakes in IPL and CPEF I Curity in one move - capturing both the fee engine and the develop-and-sell agenda. Vehicle V (IVP, ~€100m circular-infrastructure roll-up, ~€1.5m p.a. new fee income) is the first strategy of many, not the end state. From that base, strategies are added in the sequence the top 20 US and European managers have proven - credit and special situations first, real assets second, wealth and evergreen capital last - following the ICG / Tikehau scaling path, not the Blackstone one. Cyprus is the structurally correct domicile: RAIF umbrella, sub-fund per strategy, EU passport, 8% flat carry tax.

**DD challenge - the strategic fork.** The Project V memo's Tier 1 route (multi-affiliate distributor, majority + roll) monetises the founders but cedes control - it makes Impactus an affiliate of someone else's house. The world-class-house ambition set out here argues for Tier 3 (Kudu-style permanent-capital minority, Principal keeps control) or a Tier 1 deal negotiated with an explicit multi-strategy build-out mandate and distribution commitment. Decide the fork before outreach - it determines which buyers are approached first and what the retention package must protect.

## Implications

- **Regulatory.** CySEC change-of-control approval is the binding CP on Project V - dialogue opens now, not at signing. Full-scope AIFM needed beyond sub-threshold caps (€100m leveraged / €500m unleveraged). ELTIF 2.0 authorisation for the evergreen sleeve; CIF licence or partnership for the retail funnel; MiFID II product governance on AMC wrappers; A2 stays under Greek Law 4139/2013 licensing; Cyprus NPL purchases need a CBC-licensed credit acquirer or servicer partnership.
- **Tax.** Cyprus 12.5% CIT, NID on new equity, 8% flat carry regime for AIFM executives (10-yr election) - a structural margin advantage vs Greece and the UK. Transfer pricing on the SVC - M related-party agreement per Circular 6/2023 (CUP default) - document and reprice before the data room opens; buyers will flag it.
- **Liquidity.** Drawdown funds for all illiquid strategies - no redemption risk in Phase 1. Evergreen sleeve only in Phase 2+ with gates and 2-yr minimum holds (EQT Nexus design). Retail funnel stays in listed / T-bill product. Every develop-for-exit asset underwritten with two named exit channels. H 7% bond resolved before process launch - it is FRE drag and a buyer flag.
