# ICP library

Standing ICPs per book. These are **drafts pending K's confirmation** - the
size bands and disqualifiers are inferred from the platform's stated mandates,
not from a written investment policy. Confirm the bracketed figures against the
fund documents before a list is built on them, and update this file when they
are settled so the next run starts from fact.

The value of a library is the disqualifier lines. Fit criteria are easy to
regenerate; disqualifiers encode what was learned from wasted effort.

---

## Book 1 - Vertu Projects: fiduciary, tax and licensing services

**Who:** companies and their advisers with a Cyprus nexus and an unmet
administration, substance or licensing need.

| Dimension | Filter |
|---|---|
| Jurisdiction | Cyprus nexus: incorporated, redomiciling, or holding a Cyprus subsidiary. Secondary: Greece, wider EU, and CIS-origin groups restructuring into the EU |
| Segments | (a) groups redomiciling or establishing EU substance; (b) entities applying for a CySEC, EMI or insurance licence; (c) companies with a filed-compliance gap; (d) funds and SPVs needing administration |
| Size | [confirm] revenue €1m-€50m, or any size where the Cyprus entity is a holding or licensing vehicle |
| Trigger | Late annual return, auditor or secretary change, licence application in progress, ASP provider lost, new inbound redomiciliation |
| Buying unit | Beneficial owner, group CFO, or the referring adviser - lawyer, auditor, family office |
| Disqualifiers | Shell entities with no filed activity; any sector or ownership that fails sanctions or adverse-media screening; existing Vertu clients; entities whose current provider is a referral partner |

Note that the adviser channel converts better than the direct one and carries
less ePrivacy exposure, because introductions come warm. Weight it accordingly.

## Book 2 - Impactus deal sourcing

Buy-side. This is sourcing targets to invest in or acquire, not selling
anything, which is why it sits outside Gate 1.

### 2a - PE Fund

| Dimension | Filter |
|---|---|
| Jurisdiction | Cyprus, Greece, [confirm wider EU scope] |
| Sector | [confirm mandate - the 8% net return target implies yield-supported or cash-generative assets rather than venture risk] |
| Size | [confirm enterprise value band and cheque size] |
| Trigger | Succession with no filed successor; three years of declining filed margins; capex need against a granted permit; restructuring or receivership notice; a shareholder exit |
| Buying unit | Beneficial owner or family principal - not a titled executive |
| Disqualifiers | Regulated-activity targets requiring a licence the fund cannot hold; anything creating a CySEC conflict with existing holdings; auction processes already running with an adviser mandate; targets below the minimum cheque size |

### 2b - CPEF I Curity: medical cannabis

| Dimension | Filter |
|---|---|
| Jurisdiction | Cyprus, Greece, EU jurisdictions with an operative medical framework |
| Sector | Licensed cultivation, GMP processing and extraction, pharmaceutical distribution holding the relevant authorisations |
| Size | [confirm] |
| Trigger | Licence granted but capacity unbuilt; EU or national grant awarded against unfunded capex; GMP certification in progress; a licensed operator in distress |
| Buying unit | Founder or licence holder |
| Disqualifiers | **Any recreational exposure** - it contaminates the regulated thesis and the banking relationships. Unlicensed operators. Jurisdictions without an operative medical framework. Anything creating a banking or payments problem for the fund |

The licence is the asset. Filter on the permit register, not on company
descriptions - self-described cannabis companies without licences dominate any
keyword search and waste the entire qualification budget.

### 2c - Impactus Pyrolysis: waste tyre

| Dimension | Filter |
|---|---|
| Jurisdiction | Cyprus, Greece, [confirm] |
| Sector | End-of-life tyre collection and processing, licensed waste operators, producer-responsibility schemes, rubber crumb and carbon black offtake |
| Size | [confirm - feedstock tonnage is the operative metric, not revenue] |
| Trigger | Environmental permit granted; feedstock contract or PRO mandate won; landfill diversion obligation binding; an existing operator at capacity |
| Buying unit | Owner-operator; also the PRO scheme managers, who control feedstock allocation |
| Disqualifiers | No permit and no realistic path to one; no secured feedstock - **feedstock security is the binding constraint on this thesis, so it is a disqualifier and not a scoring factor**; sites without offtake access |

The environmental permit registers are a far better universe source here than any
commercial database, because the licensed operator population is small, named and
published.

## Book 3 - Capital raising and LP mapping

**Research and warm-introduction mapping only. No outbound list is built for
this book.** See Gate 1 in `references/compliance-gate.md` - AIFMD Art.30a(4)
makes automated outreach to prospective investors a regulatory problem, not a
volume problem.

| Dimension | Filter |
|---|---|
| Universe | Cyprus and Greek family offices and single-family structures; regional HNW principals; funds of funds and feeder structures with a Southeast Europe allocation; placement agents and regulated introducers |
| Qualification | Professional or well-informed investor status, evidenced - not assumed |
| Route | Warm introduction, regulated placement agent, or a CySEC-notified marketing process run with counsel |
| Output | An introduction map: who we want to reach, who can reach them, what the ask is. Not an email list |

The deliverable for this book is the path in, not the address.
