# AXIA's Buy-Side Rolodex Favours Credit Over Mega-PE

*Research note - AXIA Ventures Group counterparty map, 2015-2026. Prepared for Konstantinos Michaelides. Date: 29 Sep 2026.*

The thesis only partly holds. Over 2015-2026 AXIA Ventures was mainly a **sell-side, ECM and FIG adviser**. Its clients were Greek banks (Alpha, Eurobank, Piraeus, Attica), listed families and groups (Lamda, Terna, Quest, IDEAL) and utilities (PPC, ADMIE, AIA). It was **not an adviser on the 2015-2026 HRADF privatisation tenders**. Its one strategic HRADF mandate dates from 2012 ([Euromoney](https://euromoney.com/article/b12kjqnpghk0pg/greek-state-owned-assets-for-sale-one-careful-owner)).

AXIA's tombstones show **no link to the mega-fund PE teams that actually run Greek buyouts**: CVC Athens, Blackstone PE, BC Partners, Oaktree and KKR. What AXIA does give you is a verified, repeat counterparty relationship with about fifteen buy-side organisations:

| Buy-side group | Organisations |
|---|---|
| Credit and special situations | Davidson Kempner, H.I.G., B2Holding, Palisades, Comer |
| Real estate and hospitality platforms | Brook Lane (twice), Dimand/Premia, AZORA, Värde, M&G |
| Infrastructure and energy | Macquarie (twice), ContourGlobal |
| Sponsor-side | MidEuropa, Oak Hill Advisors, EnTrust Blue Ocean, Elikonos |
| Bank acquirers | Thrivest, Mistakidis |

Most of these were deals where AXIA advised a Greek bank or family selling to the fund, not deals where the fund hired AXIA. The practical conclusion is to use the map in tiers. The warm routes into Tier 1 run through **Alexandros Argyros and Haris Sitzoglou, now co-heads of investment banking inside Alpha Bank**, and through Alpha Bank itself as lender, co-investor or retained shareholder. Tier 2 names (CVC, Oaktree, BC Partners, Hines and others) have to be approached directly, on the merits of each asset.

## The IC skeptic's case: AXIA sold to funds far more often than it advised them

The strongest argument against the thesis comes from **the IC skeptic**. It runs as follows: "You are buying a rolodex that does not exist. AXIA is a Nicosia-Athens capital-markets house whose franchise is Greek bank recapitalisations and utility bookbuilds. Its value to funds was as the seller's adviser across the table, not as a trusted buy-side adviser inside their deal teams."

The evidence supports the skeptic on four counts.

**1. AXIA's own positioning is FIG-led.** It advertises **17 mandates in Greek and Cypriot bank capital raisings in 2013-2023**, ahead of Goldman Sachs and JP Morgan at 12 each. The table is AXIA-compiled and its source is not stated ([AXIA Corporate Profile 2026](https://www.axiavg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2026-07-23-AXIA-Ventures-Corporate-Presentation.pdf)).

**2. There is no privatisation or mega-fund evidence.** About 130 tombstones and 850-974 website posts were read. None shows a role in Egnatia Odos, Attiki Odos, ThPA/OLP, DESFA, the sale of 49% of HEDNO to Macquarie, the sale of ADMIE to State Grid, or any Cypriot state asset. The same record shows no role on Skroutz, Vivartia, Hellenic Healthcare, Pharmathen, Kaizen, Kotsovolos, Theon, or Cypriot deals such as Blue Island, Primetel or Cablenet ([AXIA transactions](https://www.axiavg.com/investment-banking/transactions/)).

On privatisations, AXIA's closest 2015-2026 roles were:

- Independent valuator to HELPE on the **DEPA Infrastructure** tender ([tombstone](https://www.axiavg.com/transactions/hpetroleum_depa_july2021/)).
- Co-lead manager on the **AIA IPO** and the **HELLENiQ** sell-down.

**3. Most fund relationships were adversarial.** In the NPL deals AXIA advised the selling bank:

- Alpha on **Galaxy** (buyer: Davidson Kempner).
- Eurobank and Piraeus on **Kipriotis** (buyer: H.I.G.).
- Eurobank on **Saracakis** (buyer: B2Holding).

In real estate it advised the seller: Lamda to Värde, Hellinikon to Brook Lane, and Alpha on Skyline to Dimand/Premia. In corporate deals it advised IDEAL against OHA and Quest against GLS.

AXIA advised the fund directly in only four confirmed cases:

| Fund | Deal | AXIA role |
|---|---|---|
| **MidEuropa** | FAMAR | Buy-side adviser |
| **Macquarie** | 50% of Enel Green Power Hellas | Buy-side adviser |
| **ContourGlobal** | Quest PV | Buy-side adviser |
| **United Overseas Group** | UACC | Buy-side adviser, financed by EnTrust |

Thrivest is a probable fifth, but the tombstone does not state the side.

**4. The platform has changed hands, and people have left.** Alpha Bank completed its 100% acquisition on **16 Dec 2025** ([AXIA/Alpha PR](https://www.axiavg.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/20251216-deltio-typou-en.pdf)). Several people who appear in the Aug-2025 corporate deck are missing from the Jul-2026 deck:

- Founder **Dimitrios Athanasopoulos**.
- The entire **Milan team**, which ran the Nexi, Elpedison and EDPR deals.
- **Sophia Pantazi**, IBD VP for renewables and real estate.

The comparison is between the [Aug-2025 deck](https://www.axiavg.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/2025-08-06-AXIA-Ventures-Corporate-Presentation.pdf) and the [Jul-2026 deck](https://www.axiavg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2026-07-23-AXIA-Ventures-Corporate-Presentation.pdf). A relationship map built on 2017-2024 deal teams therefore partly describes people who have since dispersed.

### Verdict: use AXIA as a door-opener to about ten mid-market platforms, not to mega-fund PE

The skeptic is right on mega-fund PE and wrong on relevance. The buyers AXIA knows are **exactly the buyers that fit mid-market Greek and Cypriot assets**:

- Hospitality and real estate platforms writing €50m-€300m tickets: Brook Lane, H.I.G. Realty, AZORA, Dimand.
- Renewables and BESS consolidators: Principia (Macquarie/Enel), ContourGlobal.
- Credit and special-situations funds that buy bank-originated exposures.
- Two sponsor relationships that come with a deal lead: MidEuropa (Matthew Strassberg on the FAMAR board) and OHA (up to €115m equity plus €200m of co-invest into IDEAL).

The position this report takes:

**Tier 1 (work first).** Brook Lane, H.I.G. Realty, Macquarie/Principia, AZORA, Dimand/Premia, Värde, MidEuropa, OHA, Thrivest and ContourGlobal. Approach them through Argyros or Sitzoglou, or through the Alpha Bank relationship: Alpha financed a Brook Lane LBO, keeps 35% of Skyline and kept 20% of Cepal.

**Tier 2 (approach directly).** The CVC Athens team, Oaktree, BC Partners, Hines, Invel, Fairfax, Blackstone and Masdar. Lead with specific assets, and use Delphi Forum and ATHEX conference exposure, because AXIA provides no warm path to them.

**Tier 3 (research only).** Cerberus, Bain Capital Credit, PIMCO, Brookfield and the Gulf SWFs. No named Greek lead has been identified.

For **Cypriot** assets, AXIA's own record is thin outside FIG. The Cypriot buyer pool is Brook Lane (Nicosia-HQ), Blackstone/HIP, PureHealth/HHG, ECM Partners, Medicover and Globeducate, plus the Greek banks.

## Sector-to-buyer matrix: hospitality and renewables have the deepest AXIA-proven pool

Hospitality has the most verified buyers, in both Greece and Cyprus. In 2025 Euromoney named AXIA "Greece's best investment bank for real estate and hospitality". It cited Porto Bello and Donkey/AZORA as two of only six notable institutional Greek hotel deals that year, worth about €650m in total ([Euromoney](https://www.euromoney.com/article/6esfrjram5wcc88g08c08occ8/investment-banking/awards-for-excellence-national-winners-2026-greeces-best-investment-bank-for-real-estate-and-hospitality-axia-ventures/)). This is AXIA's strongest current buyer franchise, and the right channel for any resort or city-hotel asset.

Renewables come second. AXIA sits on both sides of Macquarie's Greek platform, and ContourGlobal is a fresh entrant looking for operating PV and BESS. Cyprus BESS became investable only in 2026: the first storage contracts were signed in June and the first utility battery went live in August ([pv magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/08/11/cyprus-first-utility-battery-project-goes-online/)). Buyers there are still unproven, so a Cypriot RES asset should be pitched to Greek-platform buyers first.

Across Greece, 2025 was the busiest M&A year since 2012: **183 deals worth €20.6b** ([Grant Thornton](https://www.grant-thornton.gr/en/insights/surveys/greeces-thriving-deal-scene/)). There is competition for good assets.

## Conclusion

The evidence reframes the opportunity. AXIA's value was never privileged access to mega-fund PE committees. Its value was that for a decade it was the **Greek banks' and families' preferred counterparty for selling to mid-market specialists**. Those specialists are the credit funds that turned hotel loans into hotel ownership (H.I.G. on Kipriotis) and the real estate platforms that bought bank REO and Ellinikon land (Dimand, Brook Lane). Many of them are now asset owners that need add-ons. That makes them more receptive to off-market Greek and Cypriot assets than the CVC/Blackstone tier, which buys through structured auctions.

Two shifts follow. First, the relationship capital now sits inside **Alpha Bank**, so the warm path is institutional, not personal: Argyros and Sitzoglou come with Alpha's balance sheet, its retained stakes and its conflicts. Second, the map ages quickly. The Milan team, a founder and the real estate and renewables VP have all left since mid-2025. Two dated items are worth tracking: the **OHA co-invest window (to about 2027)** and the **Principia bolt-on programme**.
