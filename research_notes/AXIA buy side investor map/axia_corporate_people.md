# AXIA Ventures Group: corporate, PE-related, ECM and DCM mandates (2015-2026) and people

Method note: The main source is AXIA's own website. I pulled the full WordPress post archive, about 850 unique posts from 2011 to 2026, through the public `wp-json` API. I also read the press-release PDFs in its media library and three versions of the AXIA corporate presentation: Aug-2025, Jan-2026 and 23-Jul-2026.
- **Deal table:** it lists every corporate or PE-related mandate that AXIA itself announced from 2015 to 2026, plus a few from third-party sources.
- **Tombstones:** the credential slides in the corporate deck are images, so I could not extract them as text.
- **Wayback Machine:** the proxy blocked it, so I could not use archived team pages.
- **Currency:** amounts use the €k / €m / €bn suffix format.
- **Status labels:** "Verified" means the primary or press source is linked. "Unverified" means the claim is indicative only.

## Q1. Which PE funds and strategic buyers appear repeatedly across AXIA's corporate mandates?

### Takeaway
Outside banking and FIG, AXIA's announced corporate work leans heavily to ECM and sell-side or capital-raise advisory for Greek listed families and groups. The recurring clients are Lamda, Grivalia/Eurobank, Terna/GEK Terna, Alpha Bank, PPC and Quest. Classic PE buyouts are rare.
- **Only sponsor-side M&A mandates found:** MidEuropa (FAMAR, 2024, buy-side), Oak Hill Advisors as the counterparty to AXIA's client Ideal Holdings (2025), Värde Partners as the counterparty to Lamda (2017), and Macquarie Asset Management (buy-side, 2023; energy). Macquarie also bought from AXIA's client EDPR through Principia (2025).
- **No AXIA role found in the named Greek/Cypriot PE deals:** Skroutz, Vivartia, Hellenic Healthcare, Theon, Pharmathen, Kaizen, Kotsovolos, Blue Island, Papaellinas, Hermes, Primetel, Cablenet, Atlantic and the others on the list. This covers AXIA's own archive and the press I searched.

### Inferences
- **Recurring counterparties:**

- **Italian tilt:** the Nexi and HELLENiQ/Edison mandates ran through AXIA's Milan team (Sebastiani, Figus, Linari).

## Q2. Who are AXIA's senior bankers and alumni, and where are the alumni now?

### Takeaway
- **Founders:** AXIA was founded in 2008 by **George Linatsas** (ex-Piraeus Securities), **Dimitrios Athanasopoulos** and **Antonios Achilleoudis**, the latter two with New York backgrounds.
- **"Nicholas Iliopoulos":** does not appear in any AXIA source reviewed. Treat as unverified or incorrect.
- **After the Alpha Bank acquisition (closed 16-Dec-2025):** Linatsas is CEO of AXIA and Alpha Finance and Chief of Investment Banking at Alpha Bank. The investment banking division is co-headed by Alexandros Argyros and Haris Sitzoglou.
- **Alumni:** I found no published list of alumni moving to PE funds. Deck comparison shows the Milan team and several juniors left between Aug-2025 and Jul-2026, but their destinations are unverified.
