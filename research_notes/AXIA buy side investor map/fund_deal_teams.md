# Fund Deal Teams Active in Greece / Cyprus (2015-2026): People Map

Scope note: The research budget was about 25 tool calls across more than 45 funds, so coverage is uneven. CVC, Oaktree, Fortress, HIG, KKR, Hines/Henderson Park, BC Partners, Brook Lane, Macquarie, Fairfax, Latsco, Invel, Elikonos, EBRD and HDBI have sourced names. Many infrastructure and sovereign funds (ISQ, Stonepeak, Meridiam, Igneo, Ardian, Partners Group, CPP/CDPQ, Mubadala, ADQ, PIF, QIA) have no sourced names and are listed under Gaps. Names I recall from memory but could not confirm here are marked "unverified" and are kept in Gaps only.

A key source is the Newmoney article "Αυτοί είναι οι Έλληνες μάνατζερ των funds που κινούν €850 δισ." (30 Jan 2022), which lists Greek professionals at global funds: https://www.newmoney.gr/roh/palmos-oikonomias/epixeiriseis/afti-ine-i-ellines-manatzer-ton-funds-pou-kinoun-e850-dis-pics/ (cited below as [Newmoney 2022]). It is a secondary source from 2022. Titles may have changed and need re-checking on LinkedIn or firm pages. A fetch tool summarised the article, so small wording errors are possible.

## Q1. Named individuals and titles at each fund responsible for Greece/Cyprus/SEE; career history; reporting lines

### Takeaway
CVC has the deepest and best-documented local team: an Athens office led by Partner and Head of Greece Alex Fotakidis, with MD Kostis Rokas. Oaktree (Spyros Spyropoulos), Hines (Paul Gomopoulos), HIG (Stelios Theodosios), Fortress (Dean Dakolias) and BC Partners (Nikos Stathopoulos) each have one identifiable Greek "champion". Most mega-funds (Blackstone, Brookfield, Macquarie, Masdar) run Greek deals from London or regional teams without a Greek head. At those funds the public face is a non-Greek senior MD or country head.

### Inferences
- Where there is a Greek country head (CVC, Hines, Oaktree), the reporting line appears to run to the pan-European PE or real-estate leadership in London. No source states a formal reporting line for any named person.
- CVC Athens (Fotakidis, Rokas, Seretis) is the only mega-fund with a dedicated Athens PE deal team. For an adviser this makes it the most accessible buy-side team, and also a likely seller (Skroutz to Blackstone, HHG to PureHealth).
- At credit/NPL funds (Fortress, Bain Capital Credit, Davidson Kempner, Cerberus, Pimco), Greek deals seem to be run by pan-European credit teams and servicer platforms (doValue, Cepal) rather than Greek-named individuals.

### Gaps
- Most titles from [Newmoney 2022] need re-checking for 2026. People may have moved (for example Rokas, Seretis, Merkouris, Pothitou).
- The following names are from memory, not sourced in this run, and are **unverified**: the Davidson Kempner European/Greek NPL lead; the Cerberus Greece lead (Project Sky, Cyprus NPLs); the Bain Capital Credit Frontier lead; Pimco (Themis/Oxalis, Hellenic Bank APS) deal team; Elliott, Apollo, Värde, Castlelake, Round Hill and Oak Hill Greek deal leads; EOS Capital Partners, Synergia, Metron, Diorama and Velocity partners.
- No sourced Greece/Cyprus deal-team names for Brookfield (Domes Zeen Chania JV; the source returned 403), Masdar (Terna Energy; only Terna's Georgios Peristeris was retained), Mubadala, ADQ, PIF, QIA, ISQ, Stonepeak, Meridiam, Igneo, Ardian, Partners Group, CPP or CDPQ. I did not verify whether several of these funds made any Greek or Cypriot deal at all in 2015-2026.
- EIF's operational Greece team lead was not found. The EBRD Cyprus lead after the Greece/Cyprus split was not found.

## Q3. Stated current investment appetite (interviews, conference talks)

### Takeaway
Public statements from CVC (Fotakidis), Oaktree (Spyropoulos), Fairfax and BC Partners (Stathopoulos) are uniformly constructive on Greece. The main platforms are Delphi Economic Forum, THI Venture Fair and the Morgan Stanley/ATHEX Greek Investment Conference.

### Inferences

### Gaps
