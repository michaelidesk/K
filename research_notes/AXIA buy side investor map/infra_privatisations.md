# AXIA Ventures Group: Infrastructure, Energy, Privatisation and Concession Mandates (Greece, Cyprus, SEE), 2015-2026

Method note: The main source is AXIA's own tombstone archive. There are 170 transaction pages at axiavg.com/transactions/, listed in https://www.axiavg.com/transactions-sitemap.xml. Every 2015-2026 tombstone image (131 of them) was downloaded and read one by one. Each tombstone shows the client, the role, the value and the date. The body text of AXIA's press-release pages does not load when scraped, so the role details come from the tombstone images and from third-party coverage. **V** means verified, with the source URL given. **U** means unverified or inferred.

Corporate context (V): Alpha Bank bought 100% of AXIA. The preliminary agreement was signed on 31 Mar 2025, the SPA on 4 Aug 2025, and completion was on 15 Dec 2025. AXIA is now being combined with Alpha Finance and Alpha Bank's IB unit — [AXIA PR](https://www.axiavg.com/2025/12/16/alpha-bank-completes-acquisition-of-axia-creating-a-leading-investment-banking-capital-markets-platform/); [Cyprus Mail](https://cyprus-mail.com/2025/09/04/alpha-bank-moves-to-acquire-axia-ventures). ## Q1: Which infra/energy/privatisation/concession deals did AXIA advise on, and in what role?

### Takeaway
AXIA's verified record in this area is mostly **private-sector energy M&A**, plus **ECM and DCM work for the listed Greek utilities and concessionaires**: PPC, ADMIE, HELLENiQ, GEK Terna, Terna Energy and Athens International Airport (AIA). It is not a record of sell-side mandates on HRADF privatisation tenders. Its one verified strategic role with HRADF is the 2011-12 mandate as exclusive strategic adviser, shared with Houlihan Lokey (before 2015). In 2015-2026 its privatisation-linked roles were:
- independent valuator for HELPE on the DEPA Infrastructure tender (2021)
- financial adviser to HCAP, the Greek sovereign fund (2021)
- adviser to EYDAP's board (2022)
- co-lead manager on the HRADF/Paneuropean sell-down of 11% of HELLENiQ (2023)
- co-lead manager on the AIA IPO (2024)

No evidence was found that AXIA advised on Egnatia Odos, Attiki Odos, ThPA/OLP, regional ports, DESFA, DEPA Commercial, Hellinikon (the privatisation itself), Elefsina, marinas, HEDNO/DEDDIE to Macquarie, ADMIE to State Grid, or any Cyprus state asset (Cyta, EAC, ports, Vasilikos).

### Inferences
- AXIA's infra/energy franchise is **corporate M&A for international strategics and funds entering Greek renewables**: Macquarie, EDPR, ContourGlobal and Principia. It also acts as a **fairness / valuation provider** in deals among Greek conglomerates (Ellaktor, Motor Oil, Reggeborgh) and as an **ECM/DCM syndicate member** for the listed Greek utilities. It has not been a sell-side lead adviser on HRADF concession tenders. Those went to bulge-bracket and specialist firms, and none of the 2015-2026 HRADF tenders reviewed name AXIA.
- Macquarie is AXIA's most repeated infra counterparty. AXIA advised Macquarie on buying into EGP Hellas in 2023, then advised EDPR on selling to Principia (the Enel/Macquarie JV) in 2025. That puts AXIA on both sides of Macquarie's Greek renewables platform.
- Since the Alpha Bank acquisition (Dec 2025), AXIA's ECM roles on utilities (PPC, ADMIE, AIA, GEK Terna) have grown sharply in 2026. Expect more syndicate positions and fewer independent-adviser roles, because Alpha Bank is a lender to most of these groups (U).

- There is no evidence AXIA advised on Egnatia Odos, Attiki Odos, ThPA, OLP, regional ports, DESFA, DEPA Commercial, Hellenic Gas, the HEDNO 49% sale to Macquarie, the ADMIE 24% sale to State Grid, the Hellinikon privatisation, Elefsina or marinas. - Pre-2015 tombstones were not read in detail.

No losing bidders were found in public sources — [Macquarie](https://www.macquarie.com/au/en/about/news/2023/macquarie-asset-management-acquires-50-per-cent-of-enel-green-hellas.html). Jones Day advised Macquarie on legal matters; Bahas Gramatidis and Gianni & Origoni advised Enel — [Jones Day](https://www.jonesday.com/en/practices/experience/2023/07/macquarie-acquires-50-of-the-enel-green-power-hellas); [World Law Group](https://www.theworldlawgroup.com/membership/news/bahas-gramatidis-partners-greece-assisted-enel-s-p-a-for-the-sale-of-50-of-enel-green-power-hellas).
- EDPR 150MW: buyer Principia, jointly owned by Enel SpA and funds managed by Macquarie AM. Lambadarios advised EDPR on legal matters — [CBN](https://www.cbn.com.cy/article/116725/nicosia-headquartered-axia-acts-as-exclusive-financial-advisor-to-edpr-on-significant-renewable-energy-portfolio-sale-in-greece); [Lambadarios](https://www.lambadarioslaw.gr/2025/08/lambadarios-advised-edpr-on-the-successful-divestment-of-its-greek-wind-portfolio/).

The deal was among the first cleared under Greece's new FDI screening — [ContourGlobal](https://www.contourglobal.com/news/contourglobal-enters-greece-with-acquisition-of-500-mw-battery-storage-portfolio-and-37-mwp-of-operating-solar-pv/); [Balkan Green Energy News](https://balkangreenenergynews.com/contourglobal-enters-greece-with-battery-projects-small-pv-plants-purchase/).

Italgas was declared preferred bidder on 9 Sep 2021 at €733m, the SPA was signed on 10 Dec 2021 and the deal completed on 1 Sep 2022 — [SeeNews](https://seenews.com/news/greece-receives-two-binding-offers-for-gas-distributor-depa-infrastructure-747923); [HRADF](https://hradf.com/en/signing-of-the-depa-infrastructure-sale-amp-purchase-agreement-between-hradf-helpe-and-italgas/).

## Q4: Sector focus and deal-size appetite of each buyer (Greece/Cyprus)

### Takeaway
The buyers AXIA works with in Greek energy cluster around operating renewables platforms of €0.2b-€1.0b EV, plus strategic utility consolidation of €0.1b-€1.3b.

### Cited Findings
- Macquarie AM: operating and development renewables platform in Greece. It paid about €250m equity (Enel cites €345m) for 50% of EGP Hellas, EV €980m — [Macquarie](https://www.macquarie.com/au/en/about/news/2023/macquarie-asset-management-acquires-50-per-cent-of-enel-green-hellas.html); [BGEN](https://balkangreenenergynews.com/enel-sells-50-of-its-greek-green-power-subsidiary-to-macquarie/). Its appetite is small operating PV (37MWp, FiT/FiP contracted) plus a large BESS pipeline (500MW/2GWh, first 100MW/400MWh COD early 2027) — [ContourGlobal](https://www.contourglobal.com/news/contourglobal-enters-greece-with-acquisition-of-500-mw-battery-storage-portfolio-and-37-mwp-of-operating-solar-pv/).
- Motor Oil: consolidator across RES (Anemos, €1.0b EV), waste/circular (Helector, €114.7m) and infra (Ellaktor) — tombstones above.
- HELLENiQ: vertical integration into power (Elpedison, €164m+) — [HELLENiQ](https://www.helleniqenergy.gr/en/media/regulatory-announcements/completion-acquisition-50-share-capital-elpedison-bv).
- PPC: regional SEE expansion (Enel Romania, €1.24b) — [tombstone](https://www.axiavg.com/transactions/dei_october23/).
- Italgas: gas distribution (DEPA Infrastructure, €733m) — [Italgas](https://www.italgas.it/en/press-releases-price-sensitive/italgas-preferred-bidder-in-the-tender-for-the-privatization-of-depa-infrastructure-s-a/).

- For an AXIA-sourced buyer map in Greek infra/energy, the strongest verified relationships are Macquarie AM, Enel (via Principia), EDPR, ContourGlobal/KKR, Motor Oil, HELLENiQ and PPC. A second tier is state-linked through ECM: HRADF/HCAP, ADMIE, AIA, GEK Terna and EYDAP.
