Scope: Macquarie Asset Management (MAM), Davidson Kempner (DK), H.I.G. Capital / H.I.G. Realty, Brook Lane Capital, Värde Partners, MidEuropa, Oak Hill Advisors (OHA). About 25 searches/fetches. ## Q1. Macquarie Asset Management: Principia (ex-Enel Green Power Hellas, 50%, closed Dec-2023), EDPR 150MW wind (2025/26), HEDNO 49% (2021/22)

### Takeaway
The MAM person tied to Greek renewables is **Eric Bjonerud**. He is a Managing Director at MAM Green Investments and Chairman of Principia Energy. Current Principia pages indicate he still holds that seat, but I did not confirm his current MAM title on a MAM page dated 2026. **Aristotelis Chantavas** is CEO of Principia. For HEDNO, three non-Greek non-executive directors joined the board at closing (Brimont, Rakowski, Mathieson). These look like Macquarie nominees, but no public source labels them as MAM representatives.

### Cited Findings
- MAM closed the purchase of 50% of Enel Green Power Hellas on 29-Dec-2023. The company was renamed Principia Energy. It had a portfolio of about 566-577MW of wind, solar and hydro in operation or under construction. — [Greek Herald](https://greekherald.com.au/?p=128183); [Enel closing PR](https://corporate.enel.it/content/dam/enel-common/press/en/2023-december/EGP%20closing%20sale%20Hellas.pdf); [Naftemporiki EN](https://www.naftemporiki.gr/english/1631921/principia-a-joint-venture-of-macquarie-enel-green-power/)
- **Aristotelis Chantavas (Αριστοτέλης Χαντάβας)**: CEO of Principia Energy. **Eric Bjonerud**: Managing Director, MAM Green Investments, quoted at the Principia rebrand. — [Greek Herald](https://greekherald.com.au/?p=128183)
- Bjonerud is listed as **Chairman of the Board of Principia** and MD at MAM (search snippet from Principia/Workable pages). By then Principia described itself as running 71 plants / 775MW, with a 5.27GW pipeline, and as an FT1000 2026 company. The 2026 dating suggests the listing is current. — [Principia careers page (Workable)](https://apply.workable.com/principia-energy/llms.txt) (snippet; unverified on the primary page)
- In Dec-2023 Bjonerud was named one of Infrastructure Investor's "Rising Stars". His title then was Managing Director, MAM Green Investments, and he had led acquisitions totalling 1,510MW of renewables since joining Macquarie. — [Macquarie](https://www.macquarie.com/ae/en/about/company/macquarie-asset-management/general-public/insights/perspectives/macquarie-asset-management-eric-bjonerud-named-one-of-infrastructure-investors-rising-stars.html)
- EDPR/Principia deal: SPA signed 30-Jul-2025 to sell 100% of a 150MW wind portfolio (4 operating wind farms, 20-year CfDs) at an estimated EV of about €0.2B. Closed 29-Jan-2026. After the deal Principia's portfolio/pipeline stood at 727MW. — [EDPR signing](https://edpr-investors.com/en/investor-information/market-notifications/edpr-signs-asset-rotation-deal-150-mw-wind-portfolio); [EDPR closing](https://edpr-investors.com/en/investor-information/market-notifications/edpr-completes-asset-rotation-deal-150-mw-wind-portfolio); [Powergame](https://www.powergame.gr/green-power/1295172/principia-exagora-4-aiolikon-parkon-anti-200-ekat-evro/)
- Koutalidis advised on the MAM/EGP Hellas deal. K&P (Kyriakides Georgopoulos) advised Principia on the EDPR acquisition. These firms could lead to the working-level deal team. — [IFLR1000 (Koutalidis)](https://www.iflr.com/article/2gjw2n5ocpr2orkm09p9w/iflr1000/greece-koutalidis-law-firm-announces-completion-of-acquisition-deal-macquarie-asset-management-acquires-50-stake-in-enel-green-power-hellas); [IFLR1000 (K&P)](https://www.iflr.com/article/2gjw2n5ocpr2orkmyddp4/iflr1000/greece-k-p-advised-principia-energy-on-the-acquisition-of-150mw-operating-wind-farms-from-edp-renewables-in-greece)
- Principia-linked profiles surfaced by LinkedIn-indexed search: **George Koukoumelis** and **Thanos Priakos**. I could not see their roles. — [LinkedIn](https://gr.linkedin.com/in/georgekoukoumelis); [LinkedIn](https://gr.linkedin.com/in/thanos-priakos) (roles unverified)
- HEDNO: the sale closed 28-Feb-2022 for €1,320M (49%). The new board had Michail Kefalogiannis (Chair), Anastasios Manos (MD/CEO), and non-executives **Stéphane Brimont**, **Arthur Rakowski**, **Mark Mathieson**, Ourania Ekaterinari, Evangelos Aggeletopoulos, Alexandros Paterakis, Sotirios Hadjimichael, Georgia Christodoulopoulou and Konstantinos Masouras (employee representative). — [ered.gr](https://ered.gr/en/blog-1-320m-available-to-ppc-following-the-sale-of-its-stake-in-hedno-to-macquarie/)

### Inferences
- Brimont, Rakowski and Mathieson are very likely MAM nominees on the HEDNO board. From background knowledge (**unverified**): Brimont is a senior MAM Real Assets (infrastructure) executive in Europe, formerly at Engie/GDF Suez. Mathieson is a senior MAM Real Assets MD in the UK, previously CEO of SGN. Rakowski is an MAM infrastructure MD/director. Check each against current MAM or LinkedIn pages.
- Bjonerud is both the MAM deal lead and the Principia board Chair. That makes him the primary MAM coverage contact for Greek renewables and storage/hybrid assets. Chantavas is the operating-company contact.

### Gaps
- I did not find a 2026-dated MAM page confirming Bjonerud's current title. There is no public sign that he has left.
- I found no named MAM Green Investments associate or VP team members for Greece.
- I found no public source naming the MAM HEDNO deal team (2021) beyond the board appointees.

## Q2. Davidson Kempner: Project Galaxy / Cepal (2021), Attica Bank Domus/Rhodium (2024)

### Takeaway
Public deal releases name only "an entity advised by Davidson Kempner" and never identify individual deal leads for the Greek NPL/servicer deals. DK's published London leadership lists a Real Estate partner and a European Corporates bench. None of them is publicly tied to Greece.

### Cited Findings
- Galaxy: DK acquired 80% of Cepal Holdings and 51% of the mezzanine and junior notes of Alpha Bank's €10.8B Galaxy securitisation. Alpha Bank kept 20% of Cepal. — [Alpha Bank Galaxy presentation](https://www.alpha.gr/-/media/AlphaGr/Files/Group/grafeiou-typou/Galaxy-Presentation.pdf); [CEE Legal Matters](https://ceelegalmatters.com/greece/16136-zepos-yannopoulos-advises-alpha-bank-on-project-galaxy)

Expected closing was Q4-2024. — [Attica/Credia Bank PR](https://www.crediabank.com/en/group/press-office/2024/attica-bank-enters-into-a-definitive-agreement-with-davidson-kempner-for-the-disposal-of-two-portfolios-of-non-performing-exposures-domus-rhodium/); [IFLR1000 (Zepos & Yannopoulos)](https://www.iflr.com/article/2gjw2n5ocpr2orkmybz59/iflr1000/greece-zepos-yannopoulos-advises-davidson-kempner-capital-management-and-cepal-hellas-on-domus-and-rhodium-haps-securitisations)
- Other Greek DK deals: Project Pivot (Moratis Passas advised) and a residential mortgage portfolio acquisition (Zepos & Yannopoulos advised). — [CEE Legal Matters](https://ceelegalmatters.com/greece/18570-moratis-passas-advises-davidson-kempner-on-project-pivot); [IFLR1000](https://www.iflr.com/article/2glkc8oqpna1t8vp73pqp/iflr1000/greece-zepos-yannopoulos-advises-davidson-kempner-on-strategic-acquisition-of-residential-mortgage-portfolio)
- DK London leadership: **Romain Ferron**, Partner, Real Estate. **Manfredi Monticelli**, MD, Real Estate (joined 2016). **Chris Krishanthan**, Partner, European & Asian Corporates. **Daniel Boehm**, Partner, European Corporates. MDs in European Corporates: **Carlos Baigorri**, **Niklas Roehling** (joined 2016), **Naveen Sabharwal** (joined 2013) and **Benedetta Zangari** (joined 2017). — [DK team page](https://www.davidsonkempner.com/team); [Monticelli](https://davidsonkempner.com/people/manfredi-monticelli)
- The firm's senior figure is **Anthony (Tony) Yoseloff**, Executive Managing Member and CIO since Jan-2020, at DK since 1999. — [Tiger 21](https://tiger21.com/presenter/anthony-yoseloff-davidson-kempner-capital-management-llc)

### Inferences
- The Greek NPL trades are securitised-note and servicer-platform deals. They most plausibly sit with the European Corporates/credit team (Krishanthan, Boehm) or with Real Estate (Ferron, Monticelli). No public source confirms which. **Unverified.**

## Q3. H.I.G. Capital / H.I.G. Realty (Bayside): Kipriotis loans, Aldemar

### Takeaway
**Stelios Theodosiou** is now a **Managing Director, H.I.G. Real Estate (London)**. He was Principal in 2020, when H.I.G. made its Dimand investment. He is the repeat Greek-facing H.I.G. dealmaker and a regular Delphi Economic Forum speaker, which is evidence of stated Greek appetite.

### Cited Findings
- Theodosiou: Managing Director, H.I.G. Real Estate, London. He has 20+ years in real estate principal investing (NPLs/REOs, platforms, restructurings, developments). Before H.I.G. he was a Director in Deutsche Bank's European credit structuring and trading teams, a senior originator in DB real estate direct lending (special situations), and a member of DB's alternative and real assets opportunistic team. He holds a BSc from Athens University of Economics & Business and an INSEAD MBA. — [Delphi Forum NYC 2026 speaker page](https://nyc.delphiforum.gr/speakers-2026/433083151596); [DEF VIII](https://def-viii.delphiforum.gr/speaker/433083151596)
- In Jan-2020, as "Principal at H.I.G. Europe Realty", he said on the Dimand deal: "This transaction demonstrates our strong sourcing capabilities in Greece…" — [H.I.G. PR](https://hig.com/news/h-i-g-capital-invests-in-greek-property-company/)
- Aldemar: H.I.G. took control of Τουριστικές Επιχειρήσεις Νότου (the former Aldemar Rhodes hotels, 835 rooms) in 2018. It has also bought the Halyvourgia Ellados loans (about €90M) for a planned Aspropyrgos logistics hub. — [Newmoney](https://www.newmoney.gr/roh/palmos-oikonomias/business-stories/hig-to-plano-gia-to-megalo-logistics-center-ston-aspropirgo-pos-prochora-to-deal-me-veinoglou-pics/); [NavInvest](https://www.navinvestgreece.com/post/2018/07/04/hig-announces-strategic-investment-in-aldemar-hotel-group)
- Kipriotis: in Jun-2025 H.I.G. entities took control of five Kipriotis hotels on Kos, plus the conference centre, through an NPL settlement of about €140M. The hotels were leased to HotelBrain. — [Powergame](https://www.powergame.gr/akinita/1250476/ta-megala-xenodocheiaka-deals-tou-2025/)
- H.I.G. has reported total Greek investments of about €1.35B and interest in the Thessaloniki and Volos port authorities (2023). — [OT.gr](https://www.ot.gr/2023/07/04/english-edition/iig-fund-interest-in-thessaloniki-volos-port-authorities-total-investments-in-greece-of-1-35-blne)

### Inferences
- Theodosiou most likely led or co-led Aldemar, the Kipriotis loans and the Halyvourgia deals. The press names no other H.I.G. individual. **Attribution to specific deals is unverified.**

### Gaps
- I found no public names for his seniors or peers on the H.I.G. Realty Europe team, or for Bayside Capital individuals on Greek deals.
