# AXIA Ventures Group – FIG / NPL / Servicer / Real Estate / Hospitality mandates, Greece & Cyprus, 2015-2026

Method note: The main source is AXIA's own "Select Transactions" tombstone wall (https://www.axiavg.com/investment-banking/transactions/). Its images sit under https://www.axiavg.com/wp-content/uploads/YYYY/MM/<file>. I downloaded about 130 tombstones and read each one. I also read AXIA press releases through the site's WordPress API (974 posts) and some PDFs. The HTML pages are behind a bot check, but curl downloads work. "Verified" means the claim appears on an AXIA tombstone or press release, or in an exchange or press source cited inline. Currency uses the €x letter-suffix format (€10.8bn, €135m).

Key context: Alpha Bank completed its acquisition of AXIA in Dec-2025, and tombstones now carry "Member of Alpha Bank Group" ([AXIA post 16-Dec-2025](https://www.axiavg.com/2025/12/16/alpha-bank-completes-acquisition-of-axia-creating-a-leading-investment-banking-capital-markets-platform/)). Alexandros Argyros is MD & Head of Investment Banking and is quoted on Galaxy and Hellinikon. Haris Sitzoglou is MD & Head of Credit and Giovanni Sebastiani is an MD ([Galaxy PR](https://www.axiavg.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/ProjectGalaxy-PressRelease_EN.pdf); [Tyche PR](https://www.axiavg.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/291223_Project-Tyche-Press-Release-EN-vF.pdf); [Janus PR](https://www.axiavg.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/2024-12-16-Project-Janus-Press-Release-EN_vF.pdf)).

## Q1. AXIA's role in Hercules (HAPS) securitisations, bank NPL/NPE sales and servicer sales

### Takeaway
AXIA's only verified Hercules or servicer mega-deal is Alpha Bank's Project Galaxy of 2021: a €10.8bn NPE securitisation plus the sale of 80% of Cepal to Davidson Kempner. AXIA advised the seller, Alpha Bank. Its other NPL work is mid-size single-name corporate loan sales for Eurobank and Piraeus to credit funds (H.I.G., B2Holding, Palisades, Comer Group), plus one Cepal RPL sale to Optima. I found no evidence that AXIA advised on Cosmos, Phoenix, Vega, Sunrise, Frontier, Galaxy-type Cyprus deals (Helix, Echo), the KEDIPES or Hellenic Bank NPL sales, or the doValue/FPS, Intrum, Qquant, Pillarstone, Altamira or REMU transactions.

### Cited Findings
- Galaxy details: Alpha kept 20% of Cepal and signed an exclusive long-term SLA with "New Cepal" on €8.9bn of NPEs. Alpha kept 5% of the mezz/junior notes and planned to distribute 44% to shareholders. Alpha's NPE ratio in Greece was expected to fall from 43% to 24%. Argyros called it "the second largest rated NPE securitization in Europe" — [AXIA Galaxy PR](https://www.axiavg.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/ProjectGalaxy-PressRelease_EN.pdf)
- The 2019-2021 single-name sales follow one pattern. The selling bank is AXIA's client and the buyer is a credit or special-situations fund. Named buyers are H.I.G. Capital, B2Holding, Palisades and Comer Group — [AXIA transactions page](https://www.axiavg.com/investment-banking/transactions/)
- AXIA's published tombstone list for 2017-Sep-2026 has no tombstone for the other HAPS deals: Cosmos, Phoenix, Vega, Frontier, Sunrise NPE, Mexico, Solar, Pillar and Orion. It also has none for Cyprus NPL sales (Helix 1/2/3, Echo, KEDIPES/Cyprus Asset Management Co, Hellenic Bank's Project Starlight) or for the doValue/FPS, Intrum Hellas, Qquant, Pillarstone, Altamira Cyprus or REMU sales — [AXIA transactions page](https://www.axiavg.com/investment-banking/transactions/) (checked by reading all tombstones from 2017 onward)
- The file "Sunrise.png" (Apr-2021) is the Piraeus €1,380m share offering (1.2bn new shares; AXIA Joint Bookrunner). It is not the Sunrise NPE securitisation — [tombstone](https://www.axiavg.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Sunrise.png)

### Inferences
- AXIA's NPL franchise is sell-side, bank-led and mid-ticket (€48m-€135m single names). The exception is Galaxy, where AXIA was probably one of several advisers to Alpha. Other advisers were not checked, so co-adviser roles are unverified.
- Davidson Kempner, H.I.G., B2Holding, Palisades and Comer Group are the credit funds with a verified counterparty history with AXIA.
- Absence from AXIA's tombstone list strongly suggests, but does not prove, that AXIA had no advisory role on the other HAPS or servicer deals. Some mandates may never have been publicised.

### Gaps
- I found no source naming the losing or shortlisted bidders on Galaxy or on the Eurobank/Piraeus single-name sales. Debtwire-type sources were not accessible.
- The Hellenic Halyvourgia buyer is unnamed on the tombstone.
- Cepal/Optima RPL portfolio size is undisclosed. The AXIA post body is empty in the API because it is image-only.
- It is unknown whether AXIA advised any buyer (buy-side) on HAPS notes. No evidence either way.
