## 1. Which funds and strategics bought Cypriot assets, with named individuals?

### Takeaway
Cypriot deal flow since 2015 has come mainly from Greek strategics, especially the banks. Eurobank bought Hellenic Bank, Alpha bought AstroBank and AXIA, Bank of Cyprus bought the Ethniki insurers, Sklavenitis bought Papantoniou, Olympus/Hellenic Dairies bought into Kouroushis, and Trastor bought hotel/office real estate. Alongside them: Israeli and Lebanese hotel groups; a handful of global sponsors (Blackstone/GIC via HIP, Fairfax, Signal Capital, CVC-exit buyer PureHealth); Gulf and Asian strategics (Arabsat, Melco); and Maltese and Monaco telecom groups (GO plc, Monaco Telecom/NJJ). Pure-play PE control buyouts of Cypriot companies are rare. Healthcare (ECM Partners, Medicover) and education (Globeducate, AUB) are the exceptions.

### Inferences
- The most reliable repeat buyers of Cypriot assets are the Greek systemic banks and their insurance arms (Eurobank, Alpha, BoC, and NBG by implication), Greek consumer strategics (Sklavenitis, Olympus), Israeli hotel groups (Fattal, Brown, Premium Access), and Gulf capital (PureHealth, Arabsat). In a buy-side map these are "Cyprus-proven" counterparties.
- Blackstone/HIP's first Cyprus hotel suggests appetite for more resort assets. Amathus and Kanika are recycling capital and could be both sellers and buyers.
- Cypriot BESS/RES only became investable in 2026. Buyers are likely to be early-stage developers and IPPs rather than infrastructure funds for now.
- Because AXIA is now part of the Alpha Bank group, Alpha's own Cyprus acquisitions (AstroBank, the Universal Life/Altius merger) sit close to AXIA's relationships.

## 2. Which investors took part in 2024-2026 Greek processes, as winners or losing bidders?

### Takeaway
2025 was Greece's strongest M&A year since 2012, with 183 deals worth €20.6bn. The anchor buyers were cross-border strategics and Gulf capital: Euronext (ATHEX), Masdar (TERNA Energy), PureHealth (HHG) and Blackstone (Skroutz, 2026), plus Intralot outbound. Winners are well documented. Losing-bidder lists could not be verified in this pass.
