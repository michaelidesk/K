# Named Deal-Team People at AXIA Counterparties, NPL/Credit Investors and Gulf Investors Active in Greece/Cyprus (2020-2026)

Scope note: about 26 search/fetch calls. Every name below has a public source. Anything not confirmed is marked "unverified" or listed under Gaps. No personal contact data is included. Currency uses the €x letter-suffix format (for example €3.2b, €800m).

## Q1. Who are the named deal leads and Greece-coverage heads at each firm (title, career path, peers/seniors, sectors)?

### Takeaway
Named individuals are easy to find where a press release quotes executives: Azora, Blackstone, CVC/PureHealth, Masdar, ContourGlobal, Dimand/Premia, EBRD and EnTrust. For the credit/NPL funds (Cerberus, Bain Capital Credit, Fortress, PIMCO), public releases name no Greece deal leads. Only firm-level or global-leadership names came up, so those need LinkedIn or direct work. Thrivest is a three-principal vehicle with no public "investment team" beyond its partners and the management of its portfolio bank.

## Q2. What appetite for Greek/Cypriot assets did these firms state in 2024-2026?

### Takeaway
Stated appetite is strongest and most explicit at Masdar ("pumping more capital into Greece"), ContourGlobal (continued assessment in Greece), Azora (an identified acquisition pipeline for Donkey Hotels), Blackstone (Greece and SEE growth via Skroutz) and CrediaBank/Thrivest (a fifth-pillar bank and expansion into Malta). The NPL funds made no fresh public statements in this sample, although the 2024 PQH purchase shows they are still active.

### Cited Findings
- Masdar CEO Al Ramahi, 24 June 2024: "we are going to be pumping more capital into Greece and into Europe." He called Terna a base for Eastern Europe, citing Serbia, Montenegro and Poland and a "strong pipeline." — [To Vima](https://www.tovima.com/finance/masdar-more-capital-coming-to-greece-after-terna-energy-deal/amp/)
- ContourGlobal will "continue assessing further development opportunities in Greece" in renewables and storage. — [ContourGlobal](https://www.contourglobal.com/news/contourglobal-enters-greece-with-acquisition-of-500-mw-battery-storage-portfolio-and-37-mwp-of-operating-solar-pv/)
- Azora and the Ioannou family committed new capital, including an Athenaeum upgrade and "transactions from an identified acquisition pipeline." — [Azora](https://azora.com/en/blog/azora-and-the-ioannou-family-form-greek-hospitality-joint-venture/)
- Blackstone's Walsh said Skroutz is well placed to capture growth across Greece and Southeastern Europe. — [Blackstone via search](https://www.blackstone.com/news/press/blackstone-to-acquire-skroutz-greeces-leading-online-marketplace-from-cvc/)
- Thrivest/CrediaBank: Exarchou's "fifth banking pillar" SME-lending thesis. In 2025 CrediaBank was named preferred investor for 70% of HSBC Malta at about €200m. CEO Vrettou guides credit expansion of up to €1.4b and a 2026 placement. — [iefimerida](https://www.iefimerida.gr/english/greeces-thrivest-sets-plan-build-largest-non-systemic-bank); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CrediaBank); [Powergame](https://www.powergame.gr/trapezes/1400173/vrettou-crediabank-pistotiki-epektasi-os-14-dis-fetos-apo-tin-crediabank-ti-tha-krinei-ti-dianomi-merismatos/); [Newmoney](https://www.newmoney.gr/roh/palmos-oikonomias/trapezes/eleni-vrettou-to-2026-to-placement-tis-crediabank/)
- Dimand reports a pipeline of more than €1b and continues to realise value from Skyline in 2025. — [ered](https://ered.gr/real-estate-news/projects-worth-over-1b-are-in-dimand-s-pipeline)
- Fortress, Bain and Bracebridge bought €4.8b of PQH loans in August 2024. — [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/banking-law/fortress-bain-bracebridge-buy-4-8-billion-of-greek-bad-loans)
- EnTrust Blue Ocean raised a $1.3b fourth fund in 2024, and reports $7.2b deployed across 127 investments as of mid-2026. — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240429150419/en); [TradeWinds snippet](https://tradewindsnews.com/news/2-1-1085939)

### Inferences
- The Gulf capital found here (Masdar, PureHealth) acts through platform acquisitions and then adds bolt-ons. Masdar/Terna and PureHealth/HHG are therefore likely buyers for add-on renewables and healthcare assets in Greece and Cyprus. HHG already operates in Cyprus.
- The NPL funds' shift from portfolio buying (2019–22) to secondary or residual deals such as PQH suggests Greek credit appetite now focuses on residual portfolios and REO rather than large primary securitisations.

### Gaps
- No 2024–26 public statements were found from Cerberus, PIMCO or Bain on Greek or Cypriot appetite.
- PureHealth made no Greece/Cyprus follow-on statement in the sources fetched.
- No appetite statements were found from ADQ, Mubadala or QIA.
