The three tracks - and what each one requires

The gaps between the story and the paper - and the recommended approach

  1. "Boring" is not "cheap" by default - quality Cypriot SMEs are tightly held; family accounting and related-party leakage under the surface (the Karamouzis warning). Forensic QoE is the filter LPs will later pay for.
  2. Income needs the right vessel - a standard 10-year drawdown fund distributing dividends is a structural mismatch (the Rowan point). Distributing share classes / evergreen design from day one; retrofitting is painful (Petri).
  3. The club phase can drift into unauthorised fund management - deal-by-deal SPVs with investors deciding per deal, or it is an AIF (the Theocharides perimeter) - and the CySEC change-of-control file must stay clean mid-review.
  4. Operator bench is the real constraint - one MD per company or the decentralised model collapses into founder dependency; partnered beats solo by 10 IRR points.
  5. Sequencing risk - this must never delay the Project V critical path (CySEC pre-clearance, CERT, partner lock). It is the evidence layer for that process, not a competitor to it.
