Thesis. Project V - the succession buy-out of Partners A, C, D (~€13m at trough marks) - consolidates the management company, GP layer and anchor stakes in IPL and CPEF I Curity in one move: the fee engine and the develop-and-sell agenda together. Vehicle V (IVP, ~€100m circular-infrastructure roll-up, ~€1.5m p.a. new FRE, ~90% probable gated on CERT) is strategy #1 of ten, not the end state. Build follows the proven sequence - credit and special situations first, real assets second, wealth and evergreen capital last (the ICG / Tikehau path). Strategic fork to decide before outreach: Tier 1 distributor deal cedes control; the world-class-house ambition argues for a Kudu-style permanent-capital minority or a Tier 1 deal with an explicit multi-strategy build-out mandate.
