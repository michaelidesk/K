### 2.3 Pattern in the capital stacks (what lenders have actually accepted)

1. **Grant + strategic equity is the norm; senior debt is rare and always credit-enhanced.** Only Xycle (Polestar Capital senior debt with Invest-NL/InvestEU equity and Dow/Vopak strategics) and Circtec (bp €12.5m debt tied to an 8-yr offtake) show third-party debt. Source: URLs in 2.1/2.2.
2. **Offtaker-as-lender** (bp at Circtec; BASF conditional loan at Pyrum; Vitol/VTTI at Wastefront) is the dominant "debt" instrument for pyrolysis.
3. **Infra-fund equity, not infra-fund debt** (Antin at Infiniteria; Tiger at Bolder; Novo/APMH at Circtec). No infrastructure-debt fund (Section 3) has been publicly identified as lender to a tyre-pyrolysis SPV in Europe.
4. **Green bonds for FOAK pyrolysis have defaulted in the US** (Brightmark $172.5m; Fulcrum $456m creditors) — see 5.7 — which is the reference point a European lender's credit committee will cite.

(2) Plastics pyrolysis fails on offtake price, tyre pyrolysis has so far failed on licensor liquidity and JV governance (Enviro).
