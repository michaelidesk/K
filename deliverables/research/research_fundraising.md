## 1. Cyprus RAIF regime: what a RAIF may and may not do on the debt side

### 1.1 Statutory framework

| Item | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Enabling law | Law 124(I)/2018 (Alternative Investment Funds Law) enacted 31 July 2018; created the Registered AIF (RAIF). | [S] https://www.harriskyriakides.law/insights/news/registered-alternative-investment-funds/ ; CySEC consolidated text: https://www.cysec.gov.cy/en-GB/legislation/collective-investment/AIF/main-legislationn/80136/ |
| Registration, not authorisation | RAIF is not licensed by CySEC and is not under its direct prudential supervision; establishment is notified and the RAIF is entered on the CySEC register (c. 1 month). Registration does not equal authorisation. CySEC checks that the external manager's licence covers the fund's strategy before entry. | [S] https://www.harriskyriakides.law/insights/news/registered-alternative-investment-funds/ ; https://www.savvacyprus.com/cyprus-investment-funds-aif-raif-setup-guide/ ; https://www.mondaq.com/cyprus/fund-management-reits/1161542/new-guidance-by-cysec-on-the-registration-de-registration-of-registered-alternative-investment-funds |
| Manager | Must always be managed by an authorised external manager (AIFM). | [S] https://salvusfunds.com/2025/03/28/registering-a-raif-in-cyprus-in-2025/ |
| Capital | No minimum initial capital; must raise €500,000 within 12 months of registration. | [S] https://salvusfunds.com/2025/03/28/registering-a-raif-in-cyprus-in-2025/ |
| Investor base | Professional and well-informed investors only; retail prohibited; unlimited number of investors. | [S] https://www.cifacyprus.org/en/aifs ; https://philippoulaw.com/articles/alternative-investment-funds-in-cyprus |
| Well-informed investor test | Written confirmation of knowledge/experience **and** either minimum investment of **€125,000** or a positive assessment by a Cyprus bank, CIF, AIFM or UCITS ManCo. | [S] https://www.cifacyprus.org/en/aifs ; https://philippoulaw.com/articles/alternative-investment-funds-in-cyprus |
| **Loan origination** | **RAIFs are prohibited from pursuing money-market or loan-originating strategies.** Loan-originating AIFs are permissible only as CySEC-authorised AIFs (AIFUNP / AIFLNP) addressed solely to professional and/or well-informed investors, subject to CySEC-imposed requirements on risk management, leverage, disclosure and reporting. | [S] https://www.harriskyriakides.law/insights/news/registered-alternative-investment-funds/ ; https://www.cifacyprus.org/en/aifs ; https://www.mondaq.com/cyprus/fund-management-reits/1272862/cyprus-establishmentmanagement-of-a-fund-aif---raif |
| Borrowing / leverage | Sub-threshold manager limits: AUM under €100m including assets acquired through leverage, or €500m unleveraged with no redemption rights for 5 years. A RAIF managed by a full-scope AIFM has no statutory leverage cap; leverage is governed by the AIFM's Art. 15/25 AIFMD leverage limits and must be disclosed under Art. 23/24 AIFMD. | [S] https://hphlaw.eu/cyprus-establishment-management-of-a-fund-aif-raif (thresholds); [K] AIFMD Art. 15/23/24/25 mechanics |
| Issuing notes | A RAIF VCIC is a Cap. 113 company with variable capital; issuance of debt securities/notes is a matter of the constitutional documents, the offering memorandum and the AIFM's leverage policy, not a separate CySEC approval. Notes offered to the public would trigger Prospectus Regulation analysis. | [K] – confirm with Cyprus counsel |
| Umbrella / sub-funds | Umbrella RAIFs with ring-fenced compartments are permitted; annual CySEC contributions scale with number of compartments. | [S] https://salvusfunds.com/2026/02/11/understand-the-cyprus-investment-funds-aif-raif/ ; https://www.mondaq.com/cyprus/fund-management-reits/1161542/new-guidance-by-cysec-on-the-registration-de-registration-of-registered-alternative-investment-funds |
| Sector restrictions | No CySEC prohibition on medical-cannabis or waste-to-energy investments was located; the gating item is that CySEC must be satisfied the AIFM's licence covers the strategy. | [S] https://www.savvacyprus.com/cyprus-investment-funds-aif-raif-setup-guide/ (licence-coverage check); absence of prohibition = [K] |

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|

| CySEC Circular C743 (19 Dec 2025) | Required AIFMs of open-ended AIFs and UCITS ManCos to select at least two harmonised liquidity management tools and seek CySEC approval of constitutional-document amendments by 27 Feb 2026. | [S] https://www.lckfs.com/post/aifmd-ii-in-cyprus-supervision-ahead-of-legislation ; https://cxfinancia.com/aifmd-2-cyprus-fund-managers-compliance-guide/ |
| Two-tier regime | Baseline rules for any AIF that originates loans; enhanced rules for "loan-originating AIFs". | [S] https://cxfinancia.com/aifmd-2-cyprus-fund-managers-compliance-guide/ |
| Leverage caps | **175% NAV (open-ended)**; **300% NAV (closed-ended)**, commitment method. | [S] https://financialregulations.eu/blog/aifmd-2-loan-origination-requirements |
| Risk retention | AIF must retain **5%** of the notional of each loan it originates and transfers (held to maturity or 8 years, whichever is shorter). | [S] (5%) same URL; [K] (holding period) |
| Concentration | **20%** cap on loans to any single financial undertaking, AIF or UCITS. | [S] https://cxfinancia.com/aifmd-2-cyprus-fund-managers-compliance-guide/ |
| Structure | Loan-originating AIFs closed-ended by default (open-ended only if the AIFM demonstrates adequate liquidity risk management). | [S] same |
| "Loan-originating AIF" trigger | Strategy is mainly loan origination **or** notional of originated loans ≥50% of NAV. | [K] |
| Shareholder-loan carve-out | Shareholder loans ≤150% of AIF capital excluded from the leverage calculation. | [K] |
| Grandfathering | AIFs formed before 15 April 2024 that raise no new capital are deemed compliant; others have until 16 April 2029. | [K] |
| "20% diversification for retail" | Not an AIFMD II rule; the 20% figure in AIFMD II is the single-counterparty cap above. Retail diversification limits exist in ELTIF 2.0 (see 1.4). | Conflict flagged in final section |
