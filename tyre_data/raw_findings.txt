== QUALITATIVE / INSTITUTIONAL ==
[I1] Audit Office of Cyprus Special Report PE/01/2021 (GR: "Diacheirisi Apovliton Elastikon Ochimaton" / Management of Waste Vehicle Tyres), published 21 April 2021. Catalogued in the Audit Office special reports list (audit.gov.cy; datadev.ouc.ac.cy catalogue). Findings per Cyprus Times snippet: environmental-fee receipts do not itemise tyre categories and quantities; weighbridge documents record only totals by category, so individual consignments cannot be reconciled. Discussed at House Watchdog (Audit) Committee Feb 2022 (Cyprus Mail Q8).
[I2] ELT collective schemes in Cyprus: TWO licensed collective compliance systems - E4C Ltd (e4c.com.cy, non-profit; issues environmental-fee certificates and coupons per imported tyre) and RTM Tyres Recycling Ltd; individual systems also permitted. Licensed/supervised by the Department of Environment (Ministry of Agriculture, Rural Development and Environment). Sources: ETEK presentation PDF (etek.org.cy), e4c.com.cy, lappa.org/guides/epr/cyprus-epr/, repax.io/epr-in/cyprus (search snippets).
[I3] Fee mechanics: recycling/environmental fee payable per imported tyre; payment is a prerequisite for customs clearance (extra-EU). Fee varies by tyre category. Exact EUR schedule NOT obtained (e4c.com.cy not fetchable; no snippet carried amounts).
[I4] Importer register: Department of Environment producer register for tyres exists (registration obligatory for producers/first importers) but the member/importer list could NOT be extracted (moa.gov.cy blocked; search budget exhausted before targeted register search completed).
[I5] CYSTAT: publishes "Intra & Extra EU Trade Statistics (Commodity by Country)" monthly PDFs and annual "Cyprus External Trade Statistics" volumes via library.cystat.gov.cy; commodity detail in publications is at HS 6-digit level (per publication methodology snippet); CN8 detail not in the free PDFs (available via Eurostat Comext or on request from CYSTAT).
     Example files seen: library.cystat.gov.cy/NEW/INTRA_EXTRA_EU_TRADE-DEC24-120325.pdf ; library.cystat.gov.cy/NEW/Cyprus_External_Trade_Statistics-2020_VOLI-130122.pdf
